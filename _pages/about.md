I am a 3rd-year PhD student under the supervision of [Auguste Genovesio](https://www.ibens.bio.ens.psl.eu/spip.php?rubrique47) from ENS and [Valérie Mezger]( https://epigenetics.u-paris.fr/fr/valerie-mezger/) from Université de Paris Cité. My research centers on the development of advanced deep learning techniques 🤖​ for analyzing microscopy images 🔬​ associated with neurodevelopmental disorders. Specifically, I focus on designing generative models to identify subtle phenotypic variations 🔍​, which hold the potential to offer deeper insights into these conditions💡​ and in guiding the development of effective treatments 🧪​.
