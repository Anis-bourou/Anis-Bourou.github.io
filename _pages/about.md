I am a 3rd-year PhD student under the supervision of \textbf{Auguste Genovesio} from ENS and Valérie Mezger from Université de Paris Cité. My research centers on the development of advanced deep learning techniques 🤖​ for analyzing microscopy images 🔬​ associated with neurodevelopmental disorders. Specifically, I focus on designing generative models to identify subtle phenotypic variations 🔍​, which hold the potential to offer deeper insights into these conditions💡​ and in guiding the development of effective treatments 🧪​.
